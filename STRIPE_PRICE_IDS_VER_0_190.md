# Orduva Ver-0.190 — Stripe Price ID checklist

Create recurring Stripe Prices for every plan, currency and interval below, then add the IDs to Netlify environment variables.

## Plans

- Starter: 25 product store
- Growth: 50 product store
- Pro: 100+ product store

## Monthly prices

- ZAR: Starter R125, Growth R225, Pro R325
- KES: Starter KES 1,025, Growth KES 1,665, Pro KES 2,450
- GBP: Starter £6, Growth £9.50, Pro £14
- USD: Starter $8, Growth $13, Pro $19
- EUR: Starter €7, Growth €11, Pro €16

## Yearly prices

Yearly = monthly × 12 × 0.8, rounded to nearest whole number.

- ZAR: Starter R1,200, Growth R2,160, Pro R3,120
- KES: Starter KES 9,840, Growth KES 15,984, Pro KES 23,520
- GBP: Starter £58, Growth £91, Pro £134
- USD: Starter $77, Growth $125, Pro $182
- EUR: Starter €67, Growth €106, Pro €154
