# Orduva Patch Ver-0.231B — Invoice payments settings and dedicated storefront cards

Adds a Tenant Settings invoice payments section and a dedicated first storefront section for customer-entered amount payment cards.

## Key points
- Tenant Settings can enable invoice payments.
- Storefront shows customer-entered amount products in a separate first section when enabled.
- Payment cards do not use product images, variants, favourites or the normal product details popup.
- Normal product card layout and payment provider flows are preserved.
- Existing customer-entered amount checkout/payment logic remains the payment mechanism.
